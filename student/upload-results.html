1. Import results from Excel

Accepts .xlsx, .xls or .csv. Expected student columns: Admission No, Roll No, Class, Name, Father, Mother, Contact, DOB; then each subject's FA1–FA6, CCA, SA in the same sequence as your workbook. APAAR and PEN columns are detected by their headers.

The importer does not convert blank marks to zero. Blank stays blank; “Ab” is preserved as text. Check the preview before saving. The import merges the result record instead of intentionally replacing unrelated fields.
Choose a workbook, then select Preview & Validate.
